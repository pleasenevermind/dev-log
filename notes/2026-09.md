# 2026-09

## 2026-09-01

- Started on-chain indexing. Picked a small contract, pulling events over JSON-RPC with eth_getLogs. Range limits per provider are the first surprise.

## 2026-09-02

- Backfill in 2k-block chunks with a checkpoint table. A failed chunk retries without losing progress, which matters when the provider rate-limits.
- Reorg handling: store block hash with every row and rewind if the parent hash changes. Without it, a reorg leaves orphaned rows that never reconcile.

## 2026-09-03

- Decoding logs: topic0 is the event signature hash, indexed args are topics, the rest is ABI-encoded data. Wrote a decoder and checked it against a known tx.

## 2026-09-04

- Postgres schema: one table for blocks, one for events, one for checkpoints. Decimal amounts stored as numeric, never float. Learned that one the hard way.

## 2026-09-05

- Idempotency: unique constraint on (tx_hash, log_index) makes replays safe. The indexer can crash at any point and resume without duplicates.
- Throughput test: 10k events in 40s with batched inserts. Single-row inserts were 8x slower. Batch size 500 is the sweet spot before memory climbs.

## 2026-09-07

- The Graph: subgraphs are convenient but the hosted service shutdown pushed everyone to decentralised indexing. For a small contract, raw RPC plus Postgres is less machinery.
